Job Fair/Networking Checklist
=============================

Remember: You're playing the long game. Even if you don't have any immediate plans to work with/for a specific
company any time soon, it NEVER hurts to meet people and make friends at these kind of events!  You never know how things might
turn out.

Prep work
---------

* Print 10+ copies of your resume, carry them in a nice folder or similar to keep them fresh
* Research multiple target companies and learn 1-2 facts
* Prep your a 30 second pitch (e.g. your name, year, major, skill highlight, what you're looking for)
* Wear something semi-serious or business-casual
* Have a way to write down notes after conversations
* Use your phone for taking photos of business cards, company booth signs, etc.

At the Fair
-----------

* Try to hit your top 3-5 companies right away
* Be sure to investigate everything; there are always surprises and things to learn
* Smile, and make eye contact, etc.
* Lead with your short pitch
* Ask real, open-ended questions (don't ask about salary, but have an enquiry specific to the company)
* Listen more than you talk
* Take a card and write down anything interesting you learned
* Take notes on stuff that didn't go too well (learn from your mistakes)
* Ask to follow up (get email, linkedin, etc.)

After the Fact
--------------

* Send a follow-up thanks email to everyone you talked to, attach your resume
* Apply online if possible
* Update your application tracking spreadsheet or db
(recommended fields: date, company name, contact name, contact email, followup date, notes on converstion, application status)

Things to Avoid
---------------

* Talking too much (keep your pitch short, and spend more time listening)
* Spending too much time waiting in line (sometimes famous companies have this problem)
* Forgetting to get email/contact info
* Forgetting to followup with a word of thanks
* Asking basic questions about the company -- you need to show interest
* Tightening up -- it's not a performance, but instead a chance to converse and learn
