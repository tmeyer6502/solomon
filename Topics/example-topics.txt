Sample Topics
-------------

This list is provided as a starting point for potential Colloquium topics. I've categorized them under several different headings; the hope is that we can select from a wide range of difficulty levels and problem domains to keep things fresh and interesting for everyone.

* Ethical/Societal

  AI & Creativity (art/music/writing)
  How to spot AI generated text/images/audio
  Technology/screen addiction
  Online security (phishing, scams, etc.)
  What is Open Source?  What is Free Software?
  Risks and rewards of relying too strongly on AI
  
* General topics/Everyday technologies

  How does WiFi work?
  How do QR codes work
  Recommender services -- how they work and where are they found
  Search engines
  Facial recognition
  AI & Your Career
  Online scams/security
  Your car is a computer: what's going on in vehicular computing

* Lighter topics

  My favorite VS Code extensions/IDE tips
  Getting good with Git
  Comparison of available documentation/formatting approaches (Markdown, LaTex, Doxygen, VSDocman)
  Using Python/Shell scripting for automating boring tasks (possible demo)
  Beginning/intermediate operations with the command line (navigation, common commands, pipes, ssh/scp/rsync, process control, etc.)
  Basic web development/design (possibly interesting for non-graphically talented developers)
  Introduction to REST, building your own API and testing with postman/curl/wget
  Introduction to SQLite
  Overview of NoSQL databases (on-prem and cloud)

* Special topics

  "My favorite papers," let's compile a list of important milestones in CS and read/discuss several
  History of computing--in the workplace (e.g., "What was Office Automation and what was it like?")
  Job searching strategies
  Interview preparation, github/portfolio development
  Technology and the Faith: (how) are computers used in Biblical research?
  Careers in computing and computing-adjacent fields

* Deep dives

  How your CPU actually works
  Containers and virtual machines
  Virtual memory/pagetables, maybe include caching
  Garbage collection
  Automotive Computer Systems (ECUs, CAN bus, GPS, over-the-air updates, etc.)
  FP deep dive
  Type systems and lambda calculus (-> Hindley-Milner)
  Computer vision
  Building your own AI/ML models
  Webapp security (XSS, CORS, etc)
  Side-channel attacks (spectre, meltdown etc)
  System design: How to build twitter, spotify, etc.
  Game engines and/or different generation techniques (Terrain maps, city, etc.)
  Accessibility and assistive technology
  What is HCI, possible research ideas 
  Evolution of RDBMS design in 20th century, future directions
  Data engineering for massive datasets (pipelines, ETL, streaming etc)

* Student reports

  Innovative ways of handling course projects/assignments
  Present on work in progress (for senior capstone projects but also current side efforts)
  Internships -- how to get, what happens on them, and how can they be helpful
  
* Experience reports

  What first- or second-year students can expect in the program (advice to fresh/soph)
  "Here's how my job/internship interview went!"
  What my first job in technology was and what I've learned from it
  Past recollections:  What it was like working during the 80s/90s/00s
  I vibe-coded an app!  What did I learn and would I do it again?
