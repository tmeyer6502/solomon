### Semester Topics

In this directory we find a list of possible topics that are intended to thought-provoking:
is there anything here you'd like to learn about, or talk about?

These topics vary in their scope and audience; some might be suitable only for "insiders," in
the CS program, while others have a more general audience. It might be desirable to host a 
"general interest," session that could benefit our neighbors--fellow students, professors, and community
members--by sharing some of your knowledge on these topics.

Toward the end of the semester you will have the opportunity to do a 5-minute "lightning talk," on a topic
of your choice.  Perhaps some of these items here will spur your imagination!
